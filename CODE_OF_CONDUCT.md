# Code of Conduct

AURA follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember many people here are new.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or sharing someone's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, Discord, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, email the maintainer at
[dinesh210805@gmail.com](mailto:dinesh210805@gmail.com). Reports are kept private. The maintainer
may warn, remove content, or ban anyone who doesn't follow them.
