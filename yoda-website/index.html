03 / GET STARTED

From download to done.

Get up and running on an Android phone in a few simple steps.

  1. 01
    Download the official APK

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap the download button above.

  2. 02
    Install the file

    Open the downloaded APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.

  3. 03
    Set up AURA

    Open the app and follow its setup for sign-in and the permissions it needs.

AURA is an early-stage project and can make mistakes. Review its actions and avoid tasks you can’t afford to have done twice. This download is the original AURA release.
