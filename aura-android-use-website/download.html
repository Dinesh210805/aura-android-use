Five steps, in order.

Nothing here is a workaround. It’s the normal path, spread across screens that are easy to miss. Step 4 is the one everybody gets stuck on.

  1. 1

    Download the file on your phone

    Tap the download button above from the phone itself, not your computer. It’s large, so use Wi-Fi if you can.

  2. 2

    Allow installs from your browser

    When you open the file, Android says the browser isn’t allowed to install apps. Tap Settings in that message and turn the switch on. It applies to the app you downloaded with, not the whole system. You can switch it off after.

    Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps › Chrome

  3. 3

    Get past Play Protect

    Google scans apps it hasn’t seen before and may show “Unsafe app blocked” or “App scan recommended”. That flags an unknown developer, not anything found in the file. Tap More details → Install anyway.

  4. 4

    Allow restricted settings, the one everyone misses

    On Android 13 and newer, apps from outside the Play Store can’t use the Accessibility service until you allow it. AURA needs it to see and touch the screen, so without this step the app installs but can’t do anything. The dialog reads “Restricted setting — for your security, this setting is currently unavailable.”

    Do this before opening AURA’s setup. Otherwise onboarding sends you to a switch you can’t flip yet, which looks like the app is broken.

    Settings › Apps › AURA › ⋮ (top right) › Allow restricted settings

    Your phone may ask for your PIN or fingerprint. Some brands say “Allow restricted setting” or hide it under “More”. If it isn’t there, your Android version doesn’t need it.

  5. 5

    Open AURA and follow setup

    AURA asks for what it needs, one screen at a time, and explains each before the system dialog appears. It needs four things to work at all: microphone, the floating bubble, the accessibility service and its own keyboard. Last, add an AI provider key in Settings → Agent Brain. Groq and Google Gemini both have free tiers.

Some brands add their own switches.

The tell-tale symptom: everything looks enabled and the floating bubble still never appears. Find your brand.

Xiaomi · Redmi · POCO MIUI / HyperOS+

MIUI has a second, private overlay permission that Android’s switch doesn’t cover. This is the most common “the bubble doesn’t show up” report.

  1. Settings → Apps → Manage apps → AURA
  2. Tap Other permissions
  3. Turn on Display pop-up windows while running in background
  4. Also turn on Display pop-up windows if listed
  5. Back on the app page, turn on Autostart
OPPO · realme ColorOS+
  1. Settings → Apps → AURA → Allow floating windows
  2. Turn on Allow floating windows and Display over other apps
  3. Settings → Battery → App battery usage → AURA → Allow background activity
  4. In Phone Manager, enable Auto-launch for AURA
vivo · iQOO Funtouch / OriginOS+
  1. Settings → Apps → AURA → Permissions → Floating windows → Allow
  2. i Manager → App manager → Autostart manager → turn on AURA
  3. Settings → Battery → High background power consumption → allow AURA
Huawei · Honor EMUI / MagicOS+
  1. Settings → Apps → AURA → Permissions → turn on Display over other apps
  2. Settings → Battery → App launch → AURA → Manage manually
  3. Enable Auto-launch, Secondary launch and Run in background
OnePlus OxygenOS+
  1. Settings → Battery → Battery optimisation → AURA → Don’t optimise
  2. If your build has it, allow AURA in Settings → Apps → App auto-launch
Samsung One UI+
  1. Settings → Battery → Background usage limits
  2. Make sure AURA is not in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps
  3. Settings → Apps → AURA → Battery → Unrestricted
Pixel · Motorola · Nothing nothing extra+

Nothing beyond the five steps. Do make sure restricted settings is unlocked; step 4 applies to every brand.

What it asks for, and why.

Five are needed for AURA to work at all. Everything else is asked the first time a task needs it, and refusing only removes that ability.

Required

Microphone

Hear your voice commands and talk with you.

Required

Display over other apps

Show the bubble on top of whatever you’re doing.

Required

Accessibility service

See the screen and tap, swipe and type for you, only while running your tasks. Banking and payment apps are always blocked.

Required

AURA Keyboard

Types in React Native and Flutter apps where normal typing fails. No visible keys, never reads what you type, switches straight back.

Required

Notifications

Keep you posted on task progress in the background.

When needed

Contacts

Find “Amma” on the device. Numbers never leave your phone.

When needed

Notification access

Read and act on notifications. Banking and security ones are filtered out.

When needed

Photos, media and files

Find and open files when you ask.

When needed

Screen capture

See screens accessibility can’t describe. Asked once per session.

When needed

Camera, location, alarms

Flashlight, “where am I”, and exact alarms and timers.

When needed

Unrestricted battery

Keep long tasks running with the screen off.

Verify your download

Every release is signed with the same key, and each release page lists the file’s SHA-256. Compare it with yours:

# Windows (PowerShell)
Get-FileHash aura-{{ version }}.apk -Algorithm SHA256
# macOS / Linux
shasum -a 256 aura-{{ version }}.apk

Updates

Apps installed outside the Play Store can’t update themselves. AURA shows a banner when a new version is out. Install it over the old one; your settings, memory and keys stay.
